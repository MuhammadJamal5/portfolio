## EDITING NOTES (After Assets Come In)

**P01 UGC Pack:** Edit each creator as a 30-45s standalone review. Hook (3s) → Problem/context (8s) → Product intro (5s) → Demo/application (10s) → Reaction (5s) → CTA (5s). Add subtitles in your brand font. Grade: warm, slightly boosted saturation, lifted blacks.

**P02 Cinematic Hero:** Edit to music. Sequence: Reveal → Texture → Hands → Environment → Application → Skin → Final product. Total: 30s. Grade: clean, minimal, slightly desaturated highlights, warm midtones.

**P03 Before/After:** Use a hard color grade contrast — Act 1 is cool/dark (temp 4800K, -20 saturation), Act 3 is warm/bright (temp 6500K, +15 saturation). The cut between acts should land on the beat.

**P04 Spokesperson:** Cut the 3 video clips together as a 20s brand sizzle. The stills become a PDF or carousel for client presentations.

**P05 Multi-Format:** Present as a triptych — all three formats playing simultaneously on your portfolio.

**P06 B-Roll:** Edit to a 90s ambient instrumental. Cuts every 2-4 seconds, rhythm-based. Grade everything with a consistent LUT — warm filmic, your signature look.
