## THE PROBLEM WITH V1 — READ THIS FIRST

V1 looked AI because:
- She moved too smoothly and too perfectly
- Gestures were perfectly synchronized with words (real humans gesture AFTER or BEFORE they speak, not exactly with)
- Speech was too fast and too continuous — no real pauses, no breath moments
- Eye contact was too locked and too consistent
- Transitions between shots were too clean

Every prompt below is written specifically to fix these problems.

---

## VOICE DIRECTION — APPLY TO ALL SHOTS

Tell ElevenLabs / voice model:
- **Speed: SLOW. 80% of normal pace.** She is not in a hurry.
- **Add natural breath sounds** between sentences — actual inhale before starting a new thought
- **Pauses:** 0.5–1 second pause between each sentence. Real people breathe between thoughts.
- **Add 1-2 natural fillers** across the full video — words like "like", "honestly", "you know" — these make speech human
- **Vary the volume slightly** — some words louder for emphasis, then naturally quieter. Not monotone.
- **End sentences with a natural trailing off** — not sharp cuts on the last word
- **Avoid: smooth continuous delivery.** Every sentence should feel like a new thought she's having in real time, not a script she memorized.

---

## CAMERA DIRECTION — APPLY TO ALL SHOTS

- **Handheld feel:** The camera should have a very subtle, slow, natural drift — like someone holding a phone for a long time. Not shaky. Not stabilized perfectly. Think: 5% handheld wobble.
- **Framing imperfection:** She should not be perfectly centered in every shot. Slightly off-center, slightly different framing between shots — like she repositioned her phone.
- **No AI smoothness on camera movement** — if Freepik adds any camera movement, make it feel like gravity and breathing, not a gimbal.

## TRANSITIONS BETWEEN SHOTS — CRITICAL

**Do NOT use:**
- Fades
- Dissolves
- AI morphs or blends between shots
- Any smooth transition effect

**USE ONLY:**
- Hard cuts — but cut at the RIGHT moment:
  - Shot 1 → Shot 2: cut on her blink after "something"
  - Shot 2 → Shot 3: cut mid-gesture as her hand rises
  - Shot 3 → Shot 4: cut as her hand reaches toward the bottle
  - Shot 4 → Shot 5: cut right as her fingers lift from her cheek
  - Shot 5 → Shot 6: cut on her blink after "just this"
  - Shot 6: natural end, no cut needed

**Why this matters:** Cutting on a blink or mid-movement hides the edit. The viewer's eye follows the motion and doesn't register the cut. This is what makes UGC feel like one continuous take even when it's multiple clips.
