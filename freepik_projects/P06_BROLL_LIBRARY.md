## CATEGORY 1 — URBAN / ARCHITECTURE (Dubai, Cairo, Riyadh aesthetic)

### Shot 01 — Skyscraper at Golden Hour

```
Low angle wide shot looking sharply upward at the facade of a modern glass skyscraper. The camera is positioned at street level, tilted 70 degrees upward, making the building converge dramatically toward the top of the frame. The glass panels reflect a split sky: the lower portion reflects warm orange and amber sunset tones, the upper portion reflects deep blue twilight. The geometry of the building's grid structure — horizontal and vertical steel lines — creates a powerful repeating pattern receding into the sky. No people visible. Pure architecture and light. Shot feel: ARRI Alexa 65, Ultra Prime 24mm, slight lens distortion from extreme upward angle, rich film color science. 16:9 horizontal, 8K, cinematic, no digital artifacts.
```

### Shot 02 — Pedestrian Bridge at Blue Hour

```
A completely empty modern pedestrian footbridge stretching from left to right across the full frame — a clean architectural span of steel and glass. The shot is from one end of the bridge looking along its length in a strong one-point perspective that leads the eye to the far end. It is deep blue hour — that 20-minute window after sunset — the sky is a rich gradient from navy at the top to a muted electric blue at the horizon. Below the bridge through the glass and steel railing, a multi-lane highway is visible: long exposure light trails from car headlights and taillights create two flowing rivers of light — white going one direction, red going the other. The bridge itself is lit by recessed floor lighting creating a clean warm runway of light. Absolutely still, no people, no traffic on the bridge. Shot feel: ARRI Alexa, 35mm lens, 10-second long exposure look, rich cinematic blue hour palette. 16:9 horizontal, 8K, photorealistic.
```

Rhythm: cut mostly on beats but hold certain shots (Shot07, Shot12) longer for breathing room.
Color grade: one unified LUT across all 15 shots — slightly desaturated shadows, warm amber midtones, clean whites. Your signature look.
No text overlay except your name and contact at the very end.

Total: 15 shots × average 5 seconds = 75 seconds + 15-second intro/outro = 90 seconds.
