# PROJECT 03 — BEFORE / AFTER PSYCHOLOGICAL AD
# CONCEPT: Problem → Discovery → Transformation. No text needed — visuals tell everything.
# Uses character Layla or anonymous woman (face cut above eyes is fine).
# OUTPUT: 5 still images across 3 acts. Edit as a 20-30s ad.

---

## ACT 1 — THE PROBLEM
## Color direction: cool, desaturated, dark, heavy. Like a cold blue-grey Instagram filter.

### Problem Shot 01 — The Environment

```
A young Arab woman in her late 20s sitting slumped at a cluttered home desk late at night. Use character Layla if possible — olive skin, dark wavy hair — but you can cut the frame at her forehead so only hands and lower face are needed for consistency. The scene: she is in a small home office or bedroom desk area, elbows on the desk, one hand propping up her face tiredly, head resting in her palm. The desk is cluttered: laptop open casting a cold blue-white glow upward on her face (the only main light source), empty coffee cup, papers with handwritten notes, sticky notes on the side of the laptop, a phone face-down. Expression: blank exhaustion, eyes slightly glazed looking at screen, mind somewhere else. The room around her is dark — only the laptop glow and a distant city light through a small window behind creates depth. Color feel of the scene: cold blue, dark, desaturated, heavy. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, cinematic, photorealistic, this feeling should be immediately recognizable to anyone who has ever been burned out.
```

### Problem Shot 02 — The Close-Up Face

```
Same late-night scene, same cold blue laptop glow. Close-up on the woman's face — light olive skin, dark wavy hair falling loosely, slight dark circles under the eyes (not extreme, just real and relatable), skin looking a little dull and dry under the unflattering cold blue screen light. She is looking at the laptop screen, not the camera. Expression: blank, tired, on autopilot. The reflection of text or spreadsheet data is barely visible as a soft glow in her dark eyes. Framing: from shoulders up, slightly tilted angle, raw and intimate. The laptop is off-frame left but its cold light hits her from the left, creating hard shadows on the right side of her face. No product visible anywhere. This shot is pure emotional weight. Cold blue color grade baked into the lighting. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, ultra-realistic, no artificial softening.
```

---

## ACT 2 — THE DISCOVERY
## Color direction: split — cool blue on the left, warm gold beginning to enter from the right. Transitional.

### Discovery Shot — The Turning Point

```
Same woman at the same desk — but now a change has happened: the laptop has been pushed to the left side of the desk and closed halfway. A crack of early morning light is appearing through the small window behind her — it is 5am or 6am, dawn is just arriving. She has picked up a white minimalist skincare serum bottle from somewhere (desk drawer, bag, package) and is holding it in both hands at chest height, looking down at it with an expression of cautious quiet curiosity — like she's heard about this before, slightly skeptical, slightly hopeful. The scene is lit with TWO sources creating a split: the remaining cold blue of the laptop glow from the left, and the first warm golden-pink light of dawn entering from the window on the right. She is positioned in the center — the product is exactly at the dividing line between cold and warm light. She is the threshold moment. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, cinematic, photorealistic. This color split is critical — it is the visual metaphor of the whole ad.
```

---

## ACT 3 — THE TRANSFORMATION
## Color direction: warm, bright, alive, saturated. Golden hour feel. The inverse of Act 1.

### Result Shot 01 — The Person

```
Same woman — now it is later that same morning or a few weeks later. She is standing at a large apartment window, side-profile to camera, looking outside. The curtains are pulled back, warm morning sunlight is flooding in and wrapping around her. She is in a clean white linen shirt. Her posture is completely different from Act 1: she is standing straight, one hand resting lightly on the window frame, completely present and calm. She has a small natural smile — not a big performance, just quiet contentment. Her skin in the warm morning light looks genuinely good: healthy, luminous, even. There is no product in frame. The room behind her is warm and airy — plants, light wood, white walls in a warm glow. This shot is about a person who has come back to themselves. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, cinematic. Warm color grade: elevated highlights, golden midtones, rich but not dark shadows. Think: the morning after a long period of stress finally lifts.
```

### Result Shot 02 — The Face (Final Frame)

```
Close-up portrait of the same woman looking directly into camera. The frame is from shoulders up. Her expression is simply calm, present, and confident — not a big smile, not an over-performance. Just ease. She is at peace. Lighting: soft warm window light from the left creating a gentle even glow on her face. Her skin is luminous under natural light — healthy glow, natural texture visible, no filter look but genuinely beautiful. Shallow depth of field — her face sharp, the background (a warm blurred apartment interior) completely soft. She is in the same white linen shirt. She holds eye contact with the camera for a beat — this is the final frame the audience sits on. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, hyper-realistic skin, warm color temperature baked into the light. This is the shot where the product does not need to appear — her face IS the result.
```

---

## EDITING STRUCTURE

Act 1 (0:00–0:08): Shot01 (3s) → Shot02 (5s)
Cut: hard cut on a cold beat
Act 2 (0:08–0:13): Discovery Shot (5s)
Cut: slow dissolve into warm tones
Act 3 (0:13–0:28): Result Shot01 (7s) → Result Shot02 (8s, hold)

Color grade workflow:
- Act 1 nodes: Temp -800K, Saturation -25, Exposure -0.5, Blue lift in shadows
- Act 3 nodes: Temp +600K, Saturation +12, Exposure +0.3, Warm orange/amber in midtones

Text overlays (if adding): minimal, white, all lowercase — act 1: nothing. Act 2: "what if one thing changed?" Act 3: product name only.
