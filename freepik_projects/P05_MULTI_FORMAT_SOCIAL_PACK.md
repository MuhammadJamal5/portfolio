# PROJECT 05 — MULTI-FORMAT SOCIAL PACK
# CONCEPT: One campaign idea, three platform formats. Shows clients you think in content systems.
# PRODUCT: Same white glass skincare bottle with gold cap from P02.
# Uses character Layla (blurred background element only in the 16:9 shot).
# OUTPUT: 3 still images — one per format. Present them as a triptych.

---

## FORMAT 01 — 9:16 VERTICAL (Instagram Stories / Reels cover / TikTok)

```
Create a 9:16 vertical format ad creative. The composition is divided into thirds: the bottom third of the frame contains the hero skincare product — a white glass bottle with a brushed gold cap — sitting on the edge of a white marble bathroom sink. The product is on the right side of the bottom third, slightly off-center, shot at a slight angle showing its profile. The middle third shows the bathroom environment behind it: clean white subway tile wall, a chrome towel ring with a folded white towel, and from the right edge a sheer white curtain catching morning light. The top third of the frame is clean empty space — bright morning light, barely cream-white, completely clear — designed to hold 2-3 lines of text or a headline overlay. This is a rule-of-thirds ad composition with intentional negative space at top. Lighting: soft diffused morning light from the left, clean and bright. The product is perfectly sharp. Everything behind it is at a very shallow depth of field — soft but legible. 9:16 vertical, 4K, ultra-realistic product photography, luxury lifestyle aesthetic, clean and breathable composition.
```

---

## FORMAT 02 — 1:1 SQUARE (Instagram Feed / Facebook / LinkedIn)

```
Create a 1:1 perfectly square format ad creative. The white glass skincare bottle with brushed gold cap is positioned absolutely dead center in the frame, with equal space on all four sides. It sits on a surface of white Calacatta marble — the marble fills the entire square. The marble is clean with very subtle light grey veining that draws the eye toward the center product. Lighting: a single dramatic side light from the right — a sharp elegant beam that catches the gold cap with a clean specular reflection and creates one crisp clean shadow stretching to the lower left. The bottle is on a perfectly flat marble surface with no other objects. Nothing else exists in this frame. The label faces forward and is sharp. The gold cap glows. This composition is so clean it feels like the product was always meant to exist here. 1:1 square format, 4K, commercial luxury product photography, Bottega Veneta or Aesop campaign aesthetic.
```

---

## FORMAT 03 — 16:9 HORIZONTAL (YouTube pre-roll / Facebook video frame / website banner)

```
Create a 16:9 horizontal format cinematic ad creative. The frame is split into two distinct zones using depth of field. Left zone (approximately one third of the frame): the white glass skincare bottle with gold cap is positioned on a white marble counter, sharp and in perfect focus, the hero. Dramatic side light from the left catches it beautifully. Right zone (approximately two thirds of the frame): a woman — character Layla: olive skin, dark wavy hair, warm features — is present in the background. She is standing at a bathroom sink, back partially turned to camera, looking out a large window. She is completely out of focus — a warm human bokeh shape in cream and warm tones. She represents the product's user without showing a testimonial. Her presence is soft, warm, implied. The composition reads: product on the left, the life it improves on the right. Lighting: morning warm window light from the right fills the right zone, a practical bathroom counter light illuminates the left zone. The transition between the two zones is seamless and cinematic. 16:9 horizontal, 4K, ultra-realistic, shallow depth of field T1.8, cinematic, luxury campaign quality.
```

---

## HOW TO PRESENT THIS IN YOUR PORTFOLIO

Show all three formats side by side as a triptych — 9:16 on the left, 1:1 in the center, 16:9 on the right — all at the same visual scale. Beneath them: "One brief. Three platforms. Zero rework." This demonstrates strategic thinking, not just execution.

You can also animate them on the portfolio — have them slide in from their respective edges (left, center, right) and lock into place. Framer Motion is already in your stack.
