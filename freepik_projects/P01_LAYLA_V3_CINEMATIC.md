## WHY V2 STILL LOOKED AI — AND HOW V3 FIXES IT

The mistake in V1 and V2: asking AI video to hold a full face talking to camera for long stretches.
That is where every AI video model fails — long face + mouth movement + eye contact = uncanny valley every time.

V3 solution: Layla's voice plays as VOICEOVER over beautiful B-roll shots.
Her face appears in only 3 shots, each lasting 2-3 seconds maximum.
Every other shot is: product close-up, skin macro, hands, environment, objects.
AI video is extraordinary at those. We use it for what it's great at.
